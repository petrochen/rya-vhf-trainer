sending an urgency or safety call is, in some respects, more like that raquired to send a routine
call, Just as for a routine call, the exact sequence of buttons to press varies from make to
make and model to model, but a typical sequence is something like this;

1. Press <Gall> to show the DSC menu on the screen

3. Select “All ships" from the list of available call types (Individual; Group; All shins; Distress}
* Prass <Enter> to contirm your selection, and reveal the next menu

3. Select “Urgency” or “Safety”

5. Press <Call> and <Enter> together to transmit the OSC call
